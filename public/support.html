Scout.

A weekly chart of what is worth watching, who made it, and where it streams. This page is for when something is not working.

What Scout does

  • Ranks what is trending this week, filtered by kind and genre
  • Searches films, series and people at once
  • Opens a profile for anyone in a cast or crew, and every title they worked on
  • Saves titles to a watchlist and tracks what you have watched
  • Shows which subscription services carry a title, country by country

Questions

  • Where does the data come from? Films, series, people and streaming availability all come from TMDB.
  • Do I need an account? No. Your watchlist is stored on your device and never leaves it.
  • My watchlist disappeared. It lives in this browser's storage. Clearing site data, or opening Scout in a different browser or a private window, will show an empty list.
  • A title says no service carries it. Scout only lists subscription streaming. A title available to rent or buy shows nothing here.
  • What does Scout cost? Nothing. No ads, no purchases.
